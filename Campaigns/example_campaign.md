# Campaign Configuration — Example Campaign

## Section 1: Core Offering
- **Campaign ID**: example_campaign
- **Company Name**: GrowLeads
- **Product/Service Being Campaigned**: B2B outbound as a service — we book qualified sales meetings for early-stage SaaS founders
- **Problem Solved (Buyer's Language)**: SaaS founders spending all their time building product but getting no consistent pipeline — no qualified demos, no predictable MRR growth, no outbound system
- **Cost of Inaction**: Every month without a pipeline system is MRR left on the table; SaaS competitors who figured out outbound earlier are compounding their ARR while you're still doing manual prospecting

## Section 2: System Architecture — Seeds vs Pipeline

### Seeds (Seed Database)
- **Who they are**: SaaS ecosystem figures whose audiences are full of SaaS founders — SaaS-focused VCs, accelerators, SaaS community builders, Indian SaaS founders with large followings, GTM and sales consultants who work with SaaS companies
- **Examples**: SaaSBOOMi community leads, Accel/Peak XV/Elevation partners, Indian SaaS founders (Freshworks, Chargebee, Zoho ecosystem), SaaS GTM coaches, Indian SaaS conference speakers
- **Purpose**: We don't sell to seeds. We mine their LinkedIn engagement to find SaaS founders who are active, thinking about growth, and struggling with pipeline
- **Tiers**: High (70+), Moderate (40+), Low (20+), Irrelevant (<20)

### Pipeline (Pipeline Database)
- **Who they are**: Founders and CEOs of pre-seed to seed B2B startups, primarily in India, who need qualified meetings to grow their business
- **Purpose**: These are the people GrowLeads sells to
- **Critical filter**: Must be a founder/CEO/co-founder of an actual company (not a solo consultant, not a student, not a large enterprise exec)

### Size-Conditional Bracket Rules

- **sizeTier = early (<10 employees)**: only Founder and Executive brackets qualify. Operator bracket is rejected. **Plus a legitimacy check: `lead.totalExperienceYears >= 6`** — sum of all professional jobs from LinkedIn experiences. Sub-6-year leads at <10-emp companies are rejected.
- **sizeTier = growth / sweet_spot / scale (≥10 employees)**: all three brackets qualify. No legitimacy check.
- **sizeTier = unknown**: treat as `growth` (all three, no check). Flagged for manual review.
- **Legitimacy threshold**: 6 years (the minimum). No upper bound.
- **Override**: when `directIntent = true`, both the Operator-reject and the experience-check are bypassed.

### Priority Signals (Gate 3 — applied after Gate 2 passes, never reject)

**Signal A — Company Size Tier** (from `/company.employeeCount`):
- **sweet_spot**: 101–200 employees
- **growth**: 11–100 employees
- **scale**: 201–4999 employees
- **early**: 1–10 employees (accepted, lower priority)
- **enterprise**: ≥5000 (REJECTED at Gate 1 Row 1.5, never reaches Gate 3)
- **unknown**: enrichment failed — treat as `growth`

**Signal B — Sales / Growth Hiring** (from `/company_jobs[].title`):
- **hiring_top_priority**: any open role matches `/sales|sdr|bdr|account\s*exec|cro|chief\s*revenue|vp\s*sales|head\s*of\s*sales|sales\s*director|revenue\s*lead|growth|seo|gtm|demand\s*gen/i`
- **hiring_normal**: no matching role

**Signal C — Geography Priority**:
- **priority**: lead.location matches any country/city in Section 4 "Geography — Primary"
- **secondary**: lead.location matches any country/city in Section 4 "Geography — Secondary"
- **unknown**: location empty or not in either list — flag for manual review
- (Hard-excluded geos reject at Gate 1 Row 1.6 — never reach Gate 3)

**Composite Priority Label**:
- **top**: directIntent = true OR hiring_top_priority = true OR (size = sweet_spot AND geo = priority)
- **high**: size ∈ {sweet_spot, growth} AND geo = priority
- **medium**: size ∈ {growth, scale} AND geo ∈ {priority, secondary}
- **low**: size = early, or size = unknown with geo = secondary

### Enrichment Behavior

- **Cache TTL**: 30 days per normalized `companyUrl` — matches the existing profile re-scrape cooldown
- **Cost per unique company**: 2 RapidAPI credits (1 for `/company`, 1 for `/company_jobs`)
- **Fallback on API failure**: `size_tier = unknown`, `hiring_top_priority = false`. Log the fallback for manual review.
- **When to enrich**: after Gate 1 passes, before Gate 2 bracket classification. Skip if the lead has no resolvable `companyUrl` (size_tier = unknown).

### Direct Intent (Gate 0 — runs before all gates)

Two-stage detector on Seed-post comment text:

- **Stage 1 regex** (free, always runs): `/interested/i`, `/dm\s+me/i`, `/how\s+(do\s+i|to)\s+(sign\s*up|buy|get\s+started)/i`, `/(pricing|cost|how\s+much|quote)/i`, `/(we|i|our\s+team)\s+(need|want|are\s+looking\s+for)/i`, `/(hiring|building)\s+(an\s+)?(outbound|sdr|gtm|growth|seo|agency|partner)/i`, `/anyone\s+know\s+(a|an|good|reliable)/i`, `/reach\s+out/i`, `/can\s+(you|we)\s+(help|chat|talk|connect)/i`
- **Stage 2 AI classifier** (GPT-4o-mini, runs only on ambiguous Stage 1 hits): receives the full comment text, returns `direct_intent` | `engagement` | `other`. Cost ≈ $0.001 per ambiguous comment.

**On detection (`directIntent = true`):**
- Force composite priority to `top`.
- Bypass the Gate 2 Operator-reject rule.
- Bypass the Gate 2 legitimacy check.
- Still must pass Gate 1 (B2B, not competitor, not enterprise, not hard-excluded geo).

### Pipeline Cap (Gate 4)

- **Max leads per company**: 2.
- **Tiebreak ranking when 3+ qualifying leads exist for the same company**:
  1. Persona bracket: `Founder > Executive > Operator`
  2. Composite priority label: `top > high > medium > low`
  3. Most recent engagement (newest wins)
- **Same-company key**: normalized `currentCompany` (lowercase, strip legal suffixes via `personalizer.js::cleanCompanyName`).
- **Dedup**: no person inserted twice — matched by `publicIdentifier` first, normalized `linkedinUrl` as backup.
